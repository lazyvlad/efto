How to pass the ogre approach

The approach has three connected stretches: the overgrown entrance, an ogre encampment and Mol’dar’s courtyard. Six stairways connect the front garden and the raised outer walk. Up and Down move through depth; Space jumps.

Ogres have separate patrols and overlapping sight. Frostweavers wear purple and carry a staff. Their blue cast mark shows where a frost bolt will go: move aside or get behind stone. Reavers chase into melee. Packs run faster than you; the raised walk also has a caster.

Sneak to keep footsteps quiet. Solid columns, camp props and walls block movement and sight. Walls stay opaque when you stand behind them. Green SAFE platforms are ruined stone perches. Walk up close, then jump onto the marked top. Packs cannot reach you there and stop casting; stay still for three seconds to make them disengage. Moving resets the timer, and jumping or dropping off removes protection. Take stairs down; dropping from the gallery costs 24 health. Dropping from a safe perch also costs health. A shouted alarm reaches nearby packmates who can hear it, rather than every ogre in the level.

C places one of three persistent frost devices on your current floor. They can slow several nearby ogres after one triggers. Z is a noisy warning shot; damage to ogres is disabled in this avoidance study.

The optional sight guide shows attention on each ogre’s current floor. The awareness meter includes raised routes. The map marks the hunter in cream, reavers in amber, frostweavers in purple and Mol’dar in gold.

Keep Mol’dar alive and lose pursuit before leaving the far arch. Retry resets the whole opening. This is a compressed platform adaptation, with no account, inventory or reward changes. Later tribute levels are not included.
