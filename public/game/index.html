How to Play

Objective

Collect as many points as you can before you die! This is I N P U T!

Controls

Desktop: Mouse to move your character

Mobile: Touch screen to move your character

Your pet: every 20 seconds it fetches one item you were not going to reach, and in between it bites harmful projectiles out of the air

ESC: Pause game. While you play the mouse is kept inside the game; Esc lets it go (this can be turned off in Settings)

S: Open/close settings

Beneficial Spells (Collect These!)

Power Word Shield - Grants temporary shield protection
Frost Nova - Freezes all projectiles (2 HP damage)
Speed Boost - Increases game speed for more points

Player Spells

Desktop: press each spell's key (shown on its button; change them under Settings, Controls) | Mobile: touch the spell icons!

Q - Dragon Cry: Move anywhere on screen + 5% crit chance (30s cooldown)
W - Zandalari: +100% points, slow projectiles and items (45s cooldown)
T - Arcane Shot: Breaks a boss's shield and hits for 3 arrows' worth; flies through projectiles (uses 5 arrows, 1.5s cooldown)
E - Autoshot: Fire 1 arrow straight up (0.5s cooldown)
R - Multishot: Fire 5 arrows in wide spread (0.5s cooldown)

Dangerous Projectiles (Avoid These!)

Fireball - 8 HP damage
Frostbolt - 15 HP damage
Shadowbolt - 10 HP damage over 5 seconds

Item Types & Points

Zee Zgnan: 15 points (Ultra Rare)
Legendary: Thunder Fury - 5 points + Auto-collect all items
Set pieces: Giantstalker, Dragonstalker and Cryptstalker armour - 6 points each, kept on your account. Every piece you own of the set you are wearing gives health, armor (hits hurt less) and attack power (arrows hit harder); each set is better than the one before
Special: 4-7 points (Rare items)
Epic: 5-10 points (Uncommon items)
Regular: 1-4 points (Common items)

Scoring Tips

• Use Zandalari spell for +20% points and to slow down all falling objects

• Collect Speed Boost projectiles to increase game speed and scoring

• Thunder Fury automatically collects all items on screen AND increases crit chance

• Save sets of your own on the character sheet, and wear the one that suits the moment: press 1, 2, 3 or 4 to change it (or tap the set panel while paused)

• Higher game speeds = more items = higher scores
